### Diff context

The change adds two optional maps (`sessionPetsEnabled: [String: Bool]?`, `sessionCap: [String: Int]?`) to the private `CustomizationPayload` Decodable, two non-optional maps to `CustomizationSnapshot`, and populates them in `read` via `payload.X ?? The reader deliberately does NOT apply default-3 or clamp `session_cap: 0`; those semantics belong to downstream consumers. Full CI is green at 708 tests (baseline 704 + 4 new).
