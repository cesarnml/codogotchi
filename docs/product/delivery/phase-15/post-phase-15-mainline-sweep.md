1. A real `WindowKey` enum (`.origin(String)`, `.session(origin:id:)`, `.combined`) parsed once at the boundary, replacing colon-split strings across the pool, factories, and menu.
2. A shared prompt/dismissal coordinator owned once and injected into both renderers, replacing the per-view monitor stacks that forced `b2820f98` to be fixed twice.
3. Converging `FloatingPetPanelManaging` / `MinimalistPanelManaging` toward one renderer protocol with two implementations — "one renderer interface, two skins" — so cross-surface parity is structural rather than manual.
